## `supplied/` — extracted from the owner's own reference build

On 2026-07-18 the product owner uploaded a working HTML prototype
(`Halo__Vadodara_11.html`, a multi-page "ae halo" bundle: `main`, `biz`,
`mission`) with 22 images embedded directly in the file. These are **not**
independently sourced, scraped, or generated by Halo's tooling — they were
supplied by the owner as part of their own reference build, so they carry a
different provenance status than everything above: **owner-supplied,
authenticity and licence unconfirmed by Halo.**

| File | Used for | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `logo-ae-halo.png` | The real "ae halo!" wordmark (script "ae" + serif "halo!" + terracotta "!") | Owner-supplied — treated as the real logo |
| `bg-hero.jpg` | Hero section background (ornate stone archway detail) | Owner-supplied, unconfirmed source/rights |
| `bg-why.jpg`, `bg-step.jpg`, `bg-vision.jpg`, `bg-work.jpg`, `bg-cta.jpg`, `bg-footer.jpg` | Per-section background photography | Owner-supplied, unconfirmed source/rights |
| `intro-photo.jpg` | Preloader background | Owner-supplied, unconfirmed source/rights |
| `gallery-*.jpg` (10 files: Laxmi Vilas Palace, Sursagar Lake, Kirti Mandir, Sayaji Baug, Baroda Museum, Maharaja Fatehsingh Museum, Tambekar Wada, Suryanarayan Temple, Kala Bhavan, Manikrao Akhada) | Heritage gallery carousel | Owner-supplied. **Note:** these are pre-designed poster/card graphics (bilingual title, ornamental border, fact captions already baked into the image), not plain photographs — reused as-is, not recreated. Source/rights of the underlying photography unconfirmed. |
| `unmapped-1.jpg`, `unmapped-2.jpg`, `unmapped-3.jpg` | Not referenced anywhere in the extracted `main`/`biz`/`mission` pages | Owner-supplied, unused so far |

**Important caveat Halo cannot verify on its own:** several of these images
(particularly `unmapped-1.jpg`, a park scene with visible people, and the
gallery poster cards) have a smooth, stylised quality consistent with
AI-generated imagery rather than straight photography. Halo has no way to
confirm which of these 22 files are real photographs vs. AI-generated
composites, or what usage rights apply to either category. **Before any of
this is used beyond an internal preview, the owner should confirm: (1)
which images are real photographs vs. generated, and (2) that Halo has the
right to publish each one.** This is the same "Verified by" gate as every
other row in this document — it just hasn't been completed yet because the
files arrived mid-session, not through the audit workflow above.

---

**Rule enforced by this file:** a status of PENDING means the corresponding
asset is not used as real Vadodara imagery anywhere in the running product,
regardless of what filename exists on disk. Only a row with every field
filled in (and a human name under "Verified by") authorises real use. The
`supplied/` table above is the interim record for owner-provided files
until that same confirmation happens.
