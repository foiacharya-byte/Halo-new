How Halo starts with you

One good recommendation saves the next person hours of doubt.

Built around real local trust. Trust before promotion — useful information first, noise later never.

  1. 01

    Someone asks

    “Good electrician in Gotri?” “Pure Jain dabba near Manjalpur?” “Quiet café to work from?” These questions already happen every day.

  2. 02

    You remember someone good

    A person, shop, café, tutor, home cook, repair guy, local maker, or business that actually helped you.

  3. 03

    You add the basics

    Name, area, contact if you have it, and why you trusted them. Less than a minute.

  4. 04

    Halo checks before showing

    We look for consent, context, duplicates, and obvious spam before anything becomes useful to others.

  5. 05

    The next person decides better

    Not because of paid ranking. Because someone local shared something real.

આપણી માટીનું · Our Vision

Vadodara is already ready.

Vadodara already has the people, places, talent, businesses, food, culture, events, makers, services, and local trust.

The problem is not that the city lacks anything.

The problem is that everything is scattered — across WhatsApp groups, saved numbers, neighbours, relatives, Instagram pages, society circles, and word-of-mouth.

Ae Halo starts by bringing trusted local discovery into one place.

But the belief is bigger: Vadodara deserves its own homegrown city companion — a place to ask, find, trust, support, participate, and belong.

Not a noisy directory. Not another generic platform.

A local layer built from inside the city, for the city.
