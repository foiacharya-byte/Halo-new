# Gujarati Review Checklist — `conversation_v1.html`

**Purpose:** a native Barodian Gujarati speaker reviews every Gujarati line in the
conversation flow *before launch*. Per `halo-gujarati-rules`: wrong or
written-feeling Gujarati breaks **વિશ્વાસ** instantly — *"Better honest English
than awkward Gujarati."*

**This is a review document only.** It does not change any copy. Nothing in this
flow ships in Gujarati until a native speaker has signed off below.

---

## How to use this

For **every line**, the reviewer ticks the six universal checks:

> ☐ **natural** — would a Barodian actually say this?
> ☐ **spoken feel** — sounds said aloud, not written
> ☐ **spelling** — matra, jodakshar, conjuncts all correct
> ☐ **tone** — warm, calm, neighbourly (not salesy, not bookish)
> ☐ **not too written** — no copywriter/essay register
> ☐ **local, not cringe** — feels of Vadodara without trying too hard

Then read the **Watch** note for the line-specific risk, and either **✅ approve**,
**✏️ suggest a fix**, or **🚩 flag for discussion**.

**Risk key:** 🟢 Low (approved/locked or trivial) · 🟡 Medium (functional or
stimulus line) · 🔴 High (carries the emotion — the lines that make or break it).

---

## ⚠️ Global decisions to settle first (affect several lines)

These four cut across many lines — decide them once, up front:

1. **The name in Gujarati script — "હાલો".** In Gujarati prose the brand is written
   **હાલો** (e.g. "હાલો ખૂલશે…"), while the wordmark/logo stays Latin `ae halo!`.
   Confirm: is **હાલો** the correct, approved Gujarati rendering of the name, or
   should the name stay Latin even inside Gujarati sentences? (Appears in G-S6a,
   G-S6c, G-E-joinSub.)
2. **English loanwords in Gujarati script.** Several lines transliterate English:
   **વૉટ્સએપ** (WhatsApp), **ઇન્સ્ટાગ્રામ** (Instagram), **જસ્ટડાયલ** (JustDial),
   **ઇલેક્ટ્રિશિયન** (electrician), **ટિફિન**, **ટ્યુશન**, **લિસ્ટ/લિસ્ટિંગ**,
   **રેટિંગ**, **વેઇટલિસ્ટ**, **બોક્સ**, **સ્પૅમ**, **ઈમેલ/AC**. Confirm each reads
   naturally (these are how people *speak* in Baroda) and that the spellings are
   the ones a Barodian would write.
3. **Gender-neutrality of self-statements.** The Stage-1 answer options were
   phrased to avoid male/female adjective endings (e.g. "અહીં નવાં છીએ"). Confirm
   they read neutral *and* natural, not awkwardly plural.
4. **The word "સ્પૅમ"** (spam) — confirm spelling/usage, or whether to keep "spam"
   in Latin.

## Lines that are intentionally English in BOTH modes (no GU to review)

For transparency — these are *not* translated, by design:
- Prelude beats 1 & 2 ("This city has always looked after its own." / "Quietly —
  name to name, door to door.") — English mood beats; the Gujarati climax follows.
  *(UX note, not a correctness item: a Gujarati-first visitor reads English here
  until the climax, then picks their language at the door. Flag if that bothers
  you.)*
- Stage 5a/5b eyebrow `ae halo` (the wordmark, Latin).
- Numerals / symbols: `4.6 ★`, `212`, `AC`, `✓`.

## Stage 1 — Landing · *recognition*

**G-S1a — Eyebrow**
- **GU:** `વડોદરા ખરેખર આમ ચાલે છે`
- **EN meaning:** "How Baroda really works."
- **Purpose:** Frames the screen as shared truth, not a question.
- **Risk:** 🟡
- **Watch:** Does "આમ ચાલે છે" sound spoken/natural as a label, or clipped?

**G-S1b — The recognition hook (HERO LINE)**
- **GU:** `સાચો માણસ ક્યારેય લિસ્ટમાં નહોતો મળતો — તમારા ભરોસાના કોઈકને એની ખબર હોય, બસ.`

- **Risk:** 🔴 (reviewer specifically wants spoken, not written, here)
- **Watch:** This is a make-or-break line. Does it sound like a Barodian *saying*
  it? Is the closing "…ખબર હોય, બસ." the right spoken cadence, or too clever? Does
  "ભરોસાના કોઈકને" feel natural?

**G-S1c — The ask**
- **GU:** `તો પહેલાં — વડોદરા તમારા માટે નવું છે, કે હંમેશાંથી ઘર રહ્યું છે?`

- **Risk:** 🟡
- **Watch:** "હંમેશાંથી ઘર રહ્યું છે" — natural, or slightly literary?

**G-S1d–g — Answer options** (gender-neutral by design — see Global #3)
- `અહીં નવાં છીએ` — "New here"
- `જનમ-ઉછેર અહીંનો જ` — "Born and raised"
- `વર્ષો પછી પાછાં ફર્યાં` — "Back after years away"
- `બસ ફરવા આવ્યાં` — "Just visiting"
- **Purpose:** Self-identification; should feel easy and warm to tap.
- **Risk:** 🟡
- **Watch:** Each must read natural *and* gender-neutral. "જનમ-ઉછેર અહીંનો જ" —
  confirm the hyphenated compound is idiomatic. "પાછાં ફર્યાં / આવ્યાં" neuter
  forms — confirm they don't sound odd for a single person.

**G-S1h — Microcopy**
- **GU:** `કોઈ સાચો-ખોટો જવાબ નથી — બસ અમે જાણીએ કે કોની સાથે વાત કરીએ છીએ.`

- **Purpose:** Reassurance; removes test anxiety.
- **Risk:** 🟡
- **Watch:** "કોની સાથે વાત કરીએ છીએ" — natural and warm, not clinical?

## Stage 5 — The reveal · *recognition* (THE line to obsess over)

- **Watch:** Does it sound like something said with affection, not stated? Is "ખબર
  જ હતી" the right spoken emphasis, or would "નામ તો તમે ક્યારનાય જાણો છો" /
