# HALO — EMOTIONAL & PRACTICAL JOBS TO BE DONE
## What Barodians are actually hiring "trust" to do
*Built on `HALO_AWARD_RESEARCH_REPORT.md`. This document is about people, not product. No design, no UI, no features — only understanding.*

---

## 0. How to read this

A "job to be done" is what someone is really trying to accomplish when they reach for help — the *progress* they want in their life, not the tool they use to get it. People don't want a directory. They want to stop feeling a certain way.

Each job below is tagged on three axes, then given a verdict:

- **FREQ** — how often a Barodian confronts this job (High / Med / Low)
- **INT** — emotional intensity when it bites (High / Med / Low)
- **GAP** — the trust deficit: how badly today's options fail it (High / Med / Low)
- **Verdict** — ✅ **Core** (Halo's reason to exist) · 🟡 **Adjacent** (can serve partially, with discipline) · ⛔ **Never** (Halo must refuse this job)

Lists are **ranked top-to-bottom by composite weight**, leaning on GAP + INT, because that intersection — *high pain, badly served* — is where loyalty and word-of-mouth are actually born.

> **The one sentence this whole document defends:**
> A Barodian almost never hires a "service finder." They hire a way to **stop feeling like a fool, an outsider, or someone with no one to ask.** The functional job is the cover story. The emotional job is the engine.

# PART A — THE 25 EMOTIONAL JOBS

*What Barodians are really chasing or avoiding, by feeling.*

### Tier 1 — The load-bearing emotional jobs (highest pain × worst served)

**A1. "Help me not get cheated — don't let me be the fool." `FREQ:High · INT:High · GAP:High` ✅ Core**
The deepest job under every service search. The painhook copy already names it: advance taken and vanished, "thoda paisa vadhare," three Tuesdays pass. Being cheated isn't a money loss — it's a *status wound*, the dread of having been naive.
*Trust gap:* there is no neutral place that tells you, before you call, whether this person has burned someone you'd believe. JustDial sells the top slot; Google reviews are gamed; WhatsApp groups are noise.
*Halo's honest limit:* it can surface whether others were burned. It can **never promise you won't be.** Over-claiming here is the one way to betray the whole premise.

**A2. "Let me feel my city is *mine* — that I belong here." `FREQ:High · INT:High · GAP:Med` ✅ Core**
પોતાપણું. The job behind "Vadodara's own." Strongest for the 40k+ MSU students and NRI returnees who have no neighbour network, and for anyone watching the city change.
*Trust gap:* national platforms make every city interchangeable; belonging can't be felt in a Justdial result.

**A3. "Let me feel I have 'a guy' — that I'm the kind of person with the right contacts." `FREQ:High · INT:High · GAP:High` ✅ Core**
In Vadodara, *"mara olkhan ma chhe"* (I know someone) is social capital. Having a trusted electrician/tiffin/tailor is a marker of being settled and competent. The job is to *own* a reliable contact, and to be the person others ask.
*Trust gap:* this knowledge lives in private heads and dies when people move; there's no way to inherit it. This is also the **social-currency share engine** — people forward "my guy" to look generous and in-the-know.

**A4. "Help me feel my time and dignity aren't being disrespected." `FREQ:High · INT:High · GAP:High` ✅ Core**
The specific fury of being stood up — *"kaale pakku aavi jaish"* and then silence. The wound is disrespect, not delay.
*Trust gap:* no consequence exists for the no-show; reputation doesn't travel, so the unreliable keep operating.

**A5. "Let me feel safe letting a stranger into my home." `FREQ:High · INT:High · GAP:High` 🟡 Adjacent**
Felt most acutely by women, families with young children, and households with elderly parents. A real, heavy, recurring job.
*Trust gap:* enormous and almost totally unserved. **But this is also a trap (see §D).** Halo can offer transparency (is this a real, vouched, claimed business?). It must **never** convert into a "verified-safe" guarantee, a background-check service, or — the dangerous version — a community/caste/gender *filter*. Serving the feeling responsibly is Adjacent; serving it naively is harm.

### Tier 2 — The identity & belonging jobs

**A6. "Let me feel proud of Vadodara." `FREQ:Med · INT:High · GAP:Med` ✅ Core**
Civic pride, sharpened by the Ahmedabad rivalry ("cleaner, calmer, cultured, *ours*"). Pride research is clear: it must be pride *we built*, never pride *against* anyone.
*Trust gap:* pride handed down by an institution is hollow; the post-flood mood made civic institutions feel untrustworthy — there's an opening for a pride that comes *from neighbours*, not authority.

**A7. "Let me feel I discovered a hidden gem." `FREQ:Med · INT:High · GAP:High` ✅ Core**
"The great tiffin aunty nobody knows." Discovery delight + bragging rights in one. High arousal → high shareability.
*Trust gap:* the gems are precisely the ones with no marketing, no listing, no SEO — invisible by definition today.

**A8. "Help me feel like a competent adult running my household." `FREQ:High · INT:Med · GAP:Med` ✅ Core**
Managing repairs, tutors, tiffin, help — "adulting." Failure here feels like personal inadequacy, not bad luck.
*Trust gap:* the labour of re-finding and re-vetting falls on the individual every single time.

**A9. "Let me feel I'm a good host — that I can give someone the taste of Vadodara." `FREQ:Med · INT:Med · GAP:Med` ✅ Core**
The lilo-chevdo gesture: feeding out-of-towners the city's own snack. Hospitality as identity.
*Trust gap:* knowing the *right* authentic source (Jagdish, Duliram, the right Mahakali) is insider knowledge newcomers and returning NRIs lack.

**A10. "Let me feel I did right by my family." `FREQ:High · INT:High · GAP:Med` ✅ Core**
The good doctor for a parent, the right tutor for a child. The emotional stakes are care and guilt, not convenience.
*Trust gap:* the cost of a wrong choice is high and the information is thin; people over-rely on one relative's opinion.

**A11. "Let me feel my elderly parents are looked after — even from far away." `FREQ:Med · INT:High · GAP:High` 🟡 Adjacent**
The NRI/retirement-city job: adult children abroad arranging help for parents in Vadodara. Intense guilt-and-love driver.
*Trust gap:* huge — remote, can't vet anyone personally. Halo can serve discovery + transparency; it must not pretend to supervise or guarantee a caregiver's conduct.

**A12. "Help me belong as a newcomer who knows no one." `FREQ:Med · INT:High · GAP:High` ✅ Core**
Students, transferees, new brides into a new pol/society. Zero inherited network; everything strangers know, they don't.
*Trust gap:* the recommendation economy is closed to outsiders — you must already know someone to get a name.

### Tier 3 — The reassurance & connection jobs

**A13. "Reassure me I'm not the only one who struggled." `FREQ:Med · INT:Med · GAP:Med` ✅ Core**
*"Haa, aa to rojnu chhe."* The relief of shared frustration; being seen.
*Trust gap:* isolation makes each person feel uniquely unlucky; nothing validates the common experience.

**A14. "Let me feel my contribution mattered — that I helped a neighbour." `FREQ:Med · INT:Med · GAP:Med` ✅ Core**
Stake-holding pride. Vouching for the good tailor is *performed care*, like a WhatsApp forward to elders is care.
*Trust gap:* current channels give no lasting credit; your recommendation scrolls away in a group and helps no one tomorrow.

**A15. "Help me trust again after being burned." `FREQ:Med · INT:High · GAP:High` ✅ Core**
The repair job. After being cheated once, people retreat to only-known-contacts and miss better options.
*Trust gap:* nothing rebuilds the willingness to try someone new; the burn calcifies into permanent suspicion.

**A16. "Let me feel modern and aspirational while staying rooted." `FREQ:Med · INT:Med · GAP:Low` ✅ Core**
The Gujlish identity — "Kem cho?" and "all good, bro" in one breath. Pride in being contemporary *and* Gujarati.
*Trust gap:* low; this is an expression job more than a deficit. Mostly a tone/positioning truth.

**A17. "Let me feel less alone in a transactional city." `FREQ:Med · INT:Med · GAP:Med` ✅ Core**
The quiet ambient job: connection, neighbourliness, the sense someone has your back.
*Trust gap:* urban anonymity; society WhatsApp groups provide some of this but also surveillance and friction.

**A18. "Let me relive and preserve old Baroda." `FREQ:Med · INT:High · GAP:Low` 🟡 Adjacent**
Nostalgia is the city's proven engagement engine (@historyofvadodara, then-vs-now). High emotional pull.
*Trust gap:* low as a *service* job; this is an attachment/identity job. Halo can honour it (the shops that survived 50 years) but it isn't a trust deficit to "solve" — it's a feeling to respect.

**A19. "Let me feel I'm supporting homegrown talent." `FREQ:Med · INT:Med · GAP:Med` ✅ Core**
Choosing the home baker, the home tiffin, the local maker over a chain. Values-aligned warmth.
*Trust gap:* home-based makers are the *least* discoverable and most easily distrusted (no shopfront, no proof they exist).

**A20. "Let me feel recognised — that my specific lane, my dialect, my world is seen." `FREQ:Med · INT:High · GAP:Med` ✅ Core**
Emotional precision: "that's MY gully, my chai kitli, my school canteen." The strongest nostalgia/share trigger.
*Trust gap:* generic platforms flatten Manjalpur and Alkapuri into identical dropdown values; no one renders *your* corner with specificity.

### Tier 4 — The quieter emotional jobs

**A21. "Help me feel hopeful that things here can actually be trustworthy." `FREQ:Med · INT:Med · GAP:High` ✅ Core**
Post-flood, post-cheating cynicism. The job is restored faith that a fair system is possible locally.
*Trust gap:* high and civilisational; every institution that disappointed deepens it.

**A22. "Let me feel my money was well spent, not wasted." `FREQ:High · INT:Med · GAP:Med` ✅ Core**
Not "cheapest" — *value*. The pendha/Jagdish loyalty proves Barodians pay premium for trusted quality.
*Trust gap:* you can't tell worth-it from waste before committing; price is visible, value is hidden.

**A23. "Let me feel connected to my society / pol / community." `FREQ:Med · INT:Med · GAP:Med` 🟡 Adjacent**
Membership in a named local group. Real and strong — but already partly owned by WhatsApp/Facebook groups, with their gatekeeping shadow.
*Trust gap:* medium; Halo can complement neighbourly trust but should not try to *become* the closed society group (that's where exclusion lives).

**A24. "Let me feel in control / less anxious before a decision." `FREQ:High · INT:Med · GAP:High` ✅ Core**
Decision confidence — the antidote to the dread of the unknown provider. The job is *calm*, not information.
*Trust gap:* more options *increase* anxiety; nothing today reduces the field to one trustworthy answer.

**A25. "Let me feel my identity as a Barodian is reflected back to me." `FREQ:Med · INT:Med · GAP:Med` ✅ Core**
The self-congruence job: "this represents who I am and where I'm from." The strongest loyalty lever a hyperlocal product has.
*Trust gap:* low as a deficit, high as an opportunity — almost nothing local reflects Barodian identity with dignity.

# PART B — THE 25 PRACTICAL JOBS

*The functional tasks. Note how thin these read without the emotional engine behind them — that's the point.*

### Tier 1 — The everyday, high-frequency, badly-served tasks

**B1. Find a reliable electrician / plumber / carpenter / AC-fridge repair near me who will actually show up. `FREQ:High · INT:High · GAP:High` ✅ Core**
*Gap:* the no-show economy; numbers from a Google search lead to voicemail or a stranger who quotes-then-vanishes.

**B2. Verify whether a provider or number is legit *before* I call. `FREQ:High · INT:High · GAP:High` ✅ Core**
*Gap:* this is the trust act itself. Today there's no pre-call verification; you find out after you've committed.

**B3. Get a provider's real contact without my number being spammed or resold. `FREQ:High · INT:Med · GAP:High` ✅ Core**
*Gap:* lead-resale platforms punish the act of enquiring; consent is nonexistent. (Halo's consent-gated contact is aimed exactly here.)

**B4. Find good tiffin / home food in my specific area. `FREQ:High · INT:Med · GAP:High` ✅ Core**
*Gap:* the best are home kitchens with no listing; discovery is pure word-of-mouth, closed to newcomers.

**B5. Find a tutor for my child — right subject, right standard, right area. `FREQ:High · INT:High · GAP:Med` ✅ Core**
*Gap:* high-stakes, thin information; reliance on one neighbour's say-so.

**B6. Find a trusted doctor / clinic / specialist. `FREQ:Med · INT:High · GAP:High` 🟡 Adjacent**
*Gap:* severe — but medical trust has a hard ceiling. Halo can list and show vouches; it must **never** imply clinical endorsement or substitute for licensing/medical judgment.

**B7. Get one trustworthy recommendation without posting in five WhatsApp groups. `FREQ:High · INT:Med · GAP:High` ✅ Core**
*Gap:* the current "system" is broadcasting a plea and sifting chaotic replies; important answers get missed in the scroll.

**B8. Find a provider who actually serves *my* locality. `FREQ:High · INT:Med · GAP:Med` ✅ Core**
*Gap:* area + landmark clarity is missing; "near MSU gate" matters more than an address.

**B25. Adjudicate who's right in a dispute / get my money back. `FREQ:Low · INT:High · GAP:High` ⛔ Never**
*Gap:* high pain, but Halo as judge/arbiter/refunder is a different, liability-soaked business that destroys neutrality.

# PART C — THE TRUST-GAP TAXONOMY

Behind all 50 jobs sit a small number of recurring trust deficits. Name these and you understand Halo's actual market:

1. **The pre-commitment blindness.** You cannot know if someone is trustworthy *until after* you've already called, paid, or let them in. Every Tier-1 job is a version of this. Halo's entire wedge is moving the trust signal to *before* the commitment.
2. **The closed recommendation economy.** Good names live in private heads and society groups. To get in, you must already be in. This locks out exactly the people with the most acute jobs — students, newcomers, NRIs, new brides.
3. **The no-memory problem.** The city's word-of-mouth has no persistence. A great recommendation helps one person once, then scrolls away. The same questions are asked forever.
4. **The gamed-signal problem.** Stars, "top-rated," sponsored slots, and glowing reviews are known to be bought or fake (68% of Indians have met fake reviews). Visible trust signals have been *poisoned* — which paradoxically makes honest, blemished, named-neighbour signals more believable.
5. **The no-consequence problem.** Reliability and unreliability both leave no trace, so the unreliable keep operating and the dependable go unrewarded.
6. **The consent/dignity breach.** Numbers get scraped, resold, spammed; enquiring is punished. The act of reaching out has itself become unsafe.
7. **The institutional-faith deficit.** Post-flood, post-cheating, faith in *systems* is low. The opening is a trust that flows *from neighbours*, not authority — bottom-up, not handed down.

# PART D — WHAT HALO MUST NEVER TRY TO SOLVE

Not "out of scope" — **actively dangerous to attempt.** These jobs are real and intense; that is exactly why naively serving them would betray the premise or cause harm.

1. **Guaranteeing a person's safety or honesty.** Halo can show whether others trusted someone. It can never certify that a stranger won't cheat, steal, or harm. The moment a badge *means* "guaranteed safe," every failure becomes Halo's liability and the honest trust posture is dead. (Jobs A1, A5, B22.)
2. **Vetting / background-checking domestic workers.** The highest-deficit job (B22) is the one to refuse most firmly. It is a legal, ethical, and caste-political minefield: "verification" of domestic labour in India slides instantly into caste/community screening, false safety guarantees, and labour exploitation. A household cook may *list herself* as a maker; Halo must never run human background checks or broker live-in help under a trust badge.
3. **Becoming a filter for *who counts* — caste, religion, community, gender as a proxy for "safe."** This is the Nextdoor/Ahmedabad-exclusion failure mode, one design decision away. Self-described attributes (a *female* tutor, a *Jain* tiffin) are legitimate preferences; a system that lets neighbours screen *people out* by identity is poison. Pride and trust must never be built *against* anyone in the city. (Jobs A5, A6, A23, B21.)
4. **Payments, escrow, refunds, disputes, arbitration.** Transacting converts a directory into a platform with liability, contradicts no-commission, and makes Halo the judge of fights it can't fairly judge. (Jobs B24, B25.)
5. **Emergency / safety dispatch.** Flood rescue, medical emergencies, crime — Halo is not 108, not the police, not a hospital. Positioning near this job risks lives and reputation. (Job B16.)
6. **Replacing professional judgment.** Medical, legal, structural-safety endorsement. Halo lists and surfaces vouches; it never substitutes for a licence. (Job B6.)
7. **A civic-grievance / anti-municipality complaints portal.** The flood made this tempting. But becoming the place to attack the VMC politicises Halo, invites it into a fight that isn't its own, and trades neutral trust for partisan heat.
8. **A pure price / deals / coupon engine.** (B23.) Wins the wrong users (price-loyal, not trust-loyal) and erodes the heritage-value feeling that actually drives Barodian loyalty.
9. **Nightlife / dating / entertainment discovery.** Not its lane; dilutes the trust identity and chases a job other platforms own.

# PART E — CHALLENGED ASSUMPTIONS

Where the obvious read is wrong:

1. **"People want more options and more reviews."** No. More options *increase* anxiety (Job A24). The job is to get to *one trustworthy answer*, fast. Abundance is the competitor's value; **decisive trust** is Halo's.
2. **"People want ratings and stars."** Stars are poisoned (Gap #4). What Barodians actually trust is the *specific neighbour's specific word* — "Nilesh from Karelibaug said he came the same evening." Identity + specificity beats any number. Halo should arguably resist the 5-star paradigm it inherited.
3. **"The job is to find a service."** The functional job is the cover story. The real job is emotional: *don't let me be the fool* (A1), *let me belong* (A2), *let me be the one with the contact* (A3). Build only the functional layer and you've built a worse Justdial.
4. **"Complaints about us mean we're failing."** The say/do gap proves the opposite: Barodians loudly complain about Garba pricing and buy out passes in hours. Complaint about a beloved thing is *participation*, not rejection. Silence is the danger sign, not noise.
5. **"Trust is built by claiming to be trustworthy."** Backwards. Trust is built by *admitting the blemish* — showing what was NOT verified as loudly as what was. A flawless directory reads as fake; an honest, incomplete one reads as real.
6. **"Newcomers and locals want the same thing."** They want opposite things. Newcomers (students, NRIs, new brides) want *orientation and belonging* — the closed economy opened up. Locals want their *existing knowledge validated* and the *social currency* of being the insider who shares it. One product, two emotional jobs.
7. **"The biggest opportunity is the biggest demand."** The maid/domestic-help job (B22) has the highest demand and deficit — and is the one Halo must most firmly refuse. Demand intensity is not permission. The strongest pull is sometimes the cliff edge.
8. **"Trust is Halo's feature."** Trust is not a feature Halo *adds*; it is the only thing Barodians are *hiring* it for. Everything else — the listings, the search, the categories — is packaging around the single job: *help me trust, without getting burned, in a city I want to feel is mine.*

# PART F — THE RANKED SYNTHESIS

If you forget the lists, keep this. The jobs that should govern every decision, by composite weight (pain × frequency × deficit):

**The five Halo exists to serve (Core, non-negotiable):**
1. **A1 — don't let me be the fool** (paired with A24 *let me feel calm before deciding* and B2 *let me verify before I call*). The trust act itself.
2. **A3 — let me be the person with the trusted contact** (the social-currency, word-of-mouth-with-memory job; the share engine).
3. **A2 / A12 — let me belong, especially when I'm new here** (the closed economy, opened).
4. **A4 — stop disrespecting my time and dignity** (accountability the city currently lacks).
5. **A7 / A19 / A20 — let me find and champion the hidden, homegrown, *recognised-as-mine* gem.**

**The five Halo must refuse (Never, on principle):**
1. Guaranteeing safety/honesty (A1's dark twin).
2. Vetting domestic workers (B22).
3. Identity-based filtering of people (the exclusion failure mode).
4. Payments / disputes / arbitration (B24, B25).
5. Emergency dispatch (B16).

> **The whole truth in one line:** Barodians are not looking for a directory. They are looking for the feeling that, in their own city, they have *someone to ask* — and won't be made a fool for asking. Serve that feeling honestly and Halo is loved. Promise more than that feeling and Halo is a liability.
