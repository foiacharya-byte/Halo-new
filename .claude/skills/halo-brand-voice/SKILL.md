# Halo Brand Voice — *આપણી ભાષા*

How Halo speaks. If copy conflicts with this file, this file wins. Pair it with
`HALO_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` — the design carries the feeling, the words carry the
trust.

---

## 0. The one thing to remember

> **Halo is a proud Barodian talking to another Barodian.**

Not a brand to a "user." Not a startup to a "market." A neighbour who knows the
city, has impeccable taste, tells you the truth, and genuinely wants you to find
the right person. Warm, human, confident, trustworthy, community-first,
emotionally intelligent.

When unsure how to phrase something, ask: *"Would a thoughtful Barodian friend
actually say this out loud?"* If no, rewrite it.

---

## 1. Tone of voice

Halo is always:
- **Warm** — friendly, never cold or transactional.
- **Human** — plain words, real rhythm, a little imperfection over polish.
- **Confident** — calm and sure; no hedging, no hype, no shouting.
- **Trustworthy** — honest about what's verified and what isn't; never overpromises.
- **Community-first** — "we," "our city," "the neighbours" — Halo belongs to Vadodara, not the other way around.
- **Emotionally intelligent** — reads the moment. Reassuring when someone's stuck, proud when something's local, gentle when something failed.

Halo is **never**:
- Corporate ("leverage," "solutions," "seamless," "empower").
- A pitch deck ("revolutionising," "disrupting," "the #1 platform," "10x").
- Marketing fluff ("amazing deals!!", "don't miss out", "limited time", emoji spam).
- Salesy, needy, or loud.

**Confidence without arrogance.** Halo doesn't brag about itself; it quietly
makes the city look good.

---

## 2. Writing style

- **Short, clear sentences.** One idea each. Read it aloud — if you run out of breath, cut it.
- **Plain English, real words.** "Find" not "discover solutions." "Tell us" not "submit your information."
- **Active and direct.** "We check every listing," not "Listings are checked."
- **Second person, warm.** "you," "your area," "your neighbours."
- **Specific over generic.** "tiffin in Alkapuri," "an electrician near MSU" — real Baroda detail beats abstract claims every time.
- **Lead-cap, light punctuation.** Sentence case almost everywhere (not Title Case Buttons). Few exclamation marks — earn them.
- **Restraint.** Say less. The most premium copy is the shortest honest version.
- **No jargon, no buzzwords, no filler.** If a word is there to sound impressive, delete it.
- **Honesty is the style.** Where something isn't verified yet, say so plainly and warmly. Trust is built in the small admissions.

---

## 3. Hero headline style

The hero is one clear, human promise — emotional or practical, never a slogan
salad. Fraunces, calm confidence, often a short terracotta emphasis on the
phrase that matters.

**Pattern:** *real local need* + *the trust payoff*. Optionally a Gujarati soul
line beneath.

✅ Good:
- "Find local services in Vadodara — without guessing **who to trust**."
- "The tiffin, the tutor, the electrician — **the ones Baroda already trusts**."
- "Vadodara's word of mouth, finally in one place."
- Sub: "Real people, real reviews, cleaner profiles — so you can choose with confidence."
- Gujarati soul line: "આપણી માટીનું." / "વડોદરામાં સાચા લોકો, સાચી માહિતી, સાચો વિશ્વાસ."

❌ Bad:
- "The #1 AI-powered hyperlocal discovery platform for Bharat 🚀"
- "Revolutionising how Vadodara finds services."
- "Your one-stop solution for all local needs!"

**Hero rules:** no "platform / solution / ecosystem," no rocket/fire emoji, no
superlatives you can't prove, AI only ever "coming soon" — never implied live.

## 4. Gujarati usage rules

(Mirrors the design system — voice edition.)

- **English carries the information; Gujarati carries the feeling.** Use Gujarati for soul lines, punchlines, hooks, signoffs — not to translate every label.
- **Never machine-translate. Never approximate.** It must read as natural, correct, idiomatic Baroda Gujarati — perfect script, spelling, matra. If unsure, keep it English. Wrong Gujarati destroys વિશ્વાસ instantly.
- **Use sparingly.** A wall of Gujarati or Gujarati on every button is gimmick. One well-placed line lands harder than ten.
- **The emotional anchors:** **વિશ્વાસ** (trust), **શોધ** (discovery/search), **પોતાપણું** (belonging). Reach for these deliberately.
- **"ae halo"** keeps its affectionate warmth ("ae halo!") — never flattened to a cold brand token.
- **Code-switch only the way Barodians actually do** — a Gujarati phrase inside English warmth, each part grammatical on its own. Don't fracture a Gujarati sentence to fit a layout.

✅ "There's a great tiffin aunty somewhere — પણ કોઈને ખબર નથી. Now there is."
❌ "વ્યવસાય શોધો" as a generic button (stiff, translated-feeling — keep "Browse the directory").

## 5. Per-surface message patterns

Each surface keeps the same warm, honest voice — only the moment changes.

### Welcome messages
Make someone feel *recognised and home*, not onboarded.
- ✅ "ae halo. Welcome — this is Vadodara's own."
- ✅ "Glad you're here. Let's find the people Baroda already trusts."
- ✅ "આવો. Vadodara's own directory — built by neighbours, for neighbours."
- ❌ "Welcome to Halo, the leading platform for local discovery. Get started!"

### Empty state messages
Always warm and honest; turn emptiness into an invitation, never a dead end.
- ✅ (no listings found) "Nothing here yet — but it's coming. Know someone good? **Add them** and help the city."
- ✅ (no reviews) "No vouches yet. **Be the first** to vouch for them."
- ✅ (new category) "This corner of Vadodara is still filling up. Add the first."
- ❌ "No results found." / "0 items." / "Oops, nothing here!"

### Search messages
Honest, helpful, local. AI is "coming soon," never live.
- ✅ Placeholder: "Try 'electrician near MSU' or 'tiffin in Alkapuri'…"
- ✅ Hint: "Search the live directory below — smart AI search is coming soon."
- ✅ (no match) "We couldn't find that one yet. Try a nearby area — or **add it** if you know it."
- ❌ "Search our AI-powered database of 10,000+ verified businesses!"
- ❌ "No results. Refine your query."

### Community messages
"We," "our city," "neighbours." Make contribution feel like pride, not a task.
- ✅ "Added by a neighbour. That's how Baroda looks out for Baroda."
- ✅ "Know a gem the city's missing? **Add them** — your one listing helps everyone."
- ✅ "Halo is built by people who live here. Thank you for adding to it."
- ❌ "Submit a business to grow our database." / "Refer & earn rewards now!"

### Error messages
Calm, human, never blame the person. Say what happened, what to do next.
- ✅ "That didn't go through — looks like the connection dropped. Mind trying once more?"
- ✅ "Something on our side slipped. We're on it — please try again in a moment."
- ✅ (form) "Just need a name and an area before we can add this — they're how neighbours find it."
- ❌ "Error 400: Bad request." / "Invalid input." / "Oops! Something went wrong 😬"

### Waitlist messages
Quiet confidence and belonging — they're early to something local and good, not on a hype funnel.
- ✅ "You're in. We'll tell you the moment Halo opens up more of Vadodara."
- ✅ "Saved. Thanks for trusting us early — we won't spam you, promise."
- ✅ "આભાર. You're among the first Barodians on Halo."
- ❌ "Congratulations! You're #4,217 in line! Share to skip ahead! 🔥"

### Directory messages
Honest about verification; proud of the local. Never imply more certainty than exists.
- ✅ "Real places, added and vouched for by people who live here."
- ✅ "Checked by a real person before it earns the Halo Trusted badge. We never publish numbers we couldn't confirm."
- ✅ Badges read plainly: "Verified by Halo," "Owner claimed," "Added by a neighbour."
- ❌ "100% verified businesses guaranteed!" / "Trusted by millions."

## 6. How Halo talks about the big themes

### Trust (વિશ્વાસ)
Earned, specific, never claimed loudly. Talk about *what was checked*, not "we're trustworthy."
- ✅ "We check every listing before it's marked trusted — and we tell you what's verified and what isn't."
- ✅ "Only neighbours who actually used the service can vouch. No paid stars, no fake praise."
- ❌ "The most trusted platform in Vadodara." / "Guaranteed safe and verified."
Rule: **show the work, don't assert the virtue.** Honesty about gaps *builds* trust.

### Discovery (શોધ)
Effortless, delightful, local. Finding *the gem next door*, not "browsing inventory."
- ✅ "The great tiffin aunty everyone mentions but nobody can find? Now you can."
- ✅ "Vadodara has someone for everything — Halo helps you find the right one."
- ❌ "Discover endless options instantly!" / "Explore our vast catalogue."
Rule: discovery is about *the right one*, not *more options*.

### Belonging (પોતાપણું)
"Ours." Local pride and recognition, warm and unforced.
- ✅ "Vadodara's own. Built by neighbours, for neighbours."
- ✅ "આપણી માટીનું — this one's ours."
- ❌ "Join our growing global community of users." / "For everyone, everywhere."
Rule: Halo is *of* Vadodara, not *for* a generic audience. Specific belongs; generic alienates.

### Local businesses
Respect and dignity. They're neighbours with a craft, never "vendors," "SKUs," or "inventory."
- ✅ "the tailor who gets the fitting right the first time," "the home baker on Akota Stadium road."
- ✅ "Local people with proper profiles, real service areas, honest reviews."
- ❌ "service providers," "merchants," "listings to monetise," "onboard vendors."
Rule: name the human craft. Specific, dignified, warm.

### Vadodara
With love and accuracy. Real areas (Alkapuri, Pratapgunj, Akota, Gotri, Sayajigunj, Karelibaug, Manjalpur, Race Course), real culture — used correctly, never as decoration.
- ✅ "from MSU gate to Manjalpur main road," "Baroda's own."
- ✅ Use "Vadodara" and "Baroda" naturally as locals do.
- ❌ Fake landmarks, wrong area names, "tier-2 city," "untapped market," anything that treats the city as data.
Rule: Vadodara is home and pride, **never** a market opportunity in tone.

## 7. Forbidden phrases (never ship)

- "platform," "solution," "ecosystem," "one-stop shop"
- "revolutionising," "disrupting," "game-changing," "next-gen," "cutting-edge"
- "leverage," "empower," "seamless," "frictionless," "synergy," "robust," "scalable"
- "the #1 / leading / best," "trusted by millions," "100% guaranteed"
- "users," "vendors," "merchants," "service providers" (say neighbours, people, the tailor, the cook…)
- "AI-powered" / any claim that AI is live (it is "coming soon")
- "don't miss out," "limited time," "hurry," "act now," "exclusive deal"
- Emoji spam, "🔥🚀💯," "Oops!", "Uh-oh!", over-punctuation "!!!"
- "tier-2 city," "untapped market," "monetise," "growth hack"

## 10. The voice test (before any words ship)

1. **Friend test:** would a thoughtful Barodian actually say this aloud to another? (Must be yes.)
2. **Corporate test:** any "platform / solution / leverage / seamless / users"? (Must be none.)
3. **Honesty test:** is every claim true and unexaggerated? Is unverified clearly unverified? AI still "coming soon"?
4. **Warmth test:** does it feel like a neighbour, not a notification?
5. **Restraint test:** is this the shortest honest version? Cut anything that's just decoration.
6. **Gujarati test:** if Gujarati is used, is it natural, correct, sparing, and earned?
7. **Belonging test:** does it sound *of* Vadodara — specific and proud — not generic and global?

If it fails the friend test or the honesty test, it does not ship.
