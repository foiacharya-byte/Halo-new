What Halo is

Vadodara has people for everything. Finding the right one is the problem.

Need an electrician, carpenter, tiffin service, tutor, tailor, mechanic, mehndi artist or home business? Most of us still rely on WhatsApp forwards, neighbour recommendations, Instagram pages or random Google listings. Sometimes it works. Many times it doesn’t.

Halo brings that scattered trust into one clean place — local people with proper profiles, service areas, timings, contact numbers and reviews that are checked before they go public. Not a noisy listing site. A community-maintained local trust system.

Halo makes Vadodara’s word of mouth searchable.

How Halo works

From a neighbour’s tip to someone you can trust

Here’s exactly what happens behind every listing — so you know what a badge means before you rely on it.

  1. 01

    A neighbour adds a place

    Someone who knows it — or the owner themselves — shares the details. Nothing is added behind anyone’s back.

  2. 02

    A real person checks it

    We confirm it exists, reach the owner for their okay, and look up official records where they apply.

  3. 03

    It earns Halo Trusted

    Only verified places get the badge — and we’re open about what we checked and what we didn’t.

  4. 04

    You find them, reach out direct

    Search, spot the badge, and contact them yourself. No middleman, no spam, no paid spots.

Our promise: no paid listings, no spam, no numbers added without the owner’s say-so. Until a listing is confirmed, it stays clearly marked “verifying.”
